Who we are

Small enough to answer the phone

Beltway Demolition is a full-service demolition contractor working across Maryland. The work is whole-structure demolition for homeowners, general contractors and developers: houses, commercial buildings, garages, sheds, pools, decks and the concrete under them, then the debris hauled off. Selective and interior demolition is available when the building itself stays.

Beltway Demolition is the demolition division of WMB LLC, a Maryland contractor based in National Harbor that holds Maryland Home Improvement Commission license #154299 and Maryland Home Builder Registration #9597. That is what allows us to act as contractor of record on licensed work, and it is the first document we hand a client who asks.

You will deal with the same person from the site walk to the final invoice. On a job where the surprises are usually underground, that matters more than an org chart.

Hazardous materials such as asbestos and lead paint are handled by licensed abatement partners, not by us; we arrange the survey and schedule their work so it does not hold up the job. See our services or the FAQ for how a project runs.
