About us

Four decades of taking industrial problems off other people's books

Meridian Industrial Group is a privately held holding company: twenty-four operating businesses, 21,400 people and $6.8 billion in annual revenue across demolition, environmental, metals, logistics and development. We buy hard assets with hard problems, and we do the work ourselves.

Who we are

Owner-operators, not brokers

Meridian began in 1986 as a single demolition crew working regional teardowns. As clients started asking who would take the site after the buildings came down, the company grew into the adjacent trades — abatement, remediation, trucking, metals processing, equipment rental — until it could carry a facility from the day it stopped producing to the day something new opened on it.

Today the group operates as twenty-four companies under common ownership, from 140 offices and regional yards in 18 countries. That structure matters on site: the excavator operator, the environmental scientist, the scale house and the project manager all answer to the same organisation, so nobody spends the schedule arguing about scope boundaries.

We are equally comfortable as a contractor delivering a fixed-scope demolition, and as a principal buying an asset outright and assuming its liabilities. Most of our work now begins as the second and ends as a piece of usable real estate.

How we operate

Four commitments we don't trade away

Safety before schedule

Structures come down in a planned sequence or they don't come down that day. No exception has ever been worth making.

Self-perform the risk

The scopes that carry the exposure — abatement, structural takedown, remediation — stay with our own crews.

Document everything

Waste manifests, sampling, closure letters. A clean file is what makes a clean site sellable years later.

Leave it better

A vacant lot is not a finish line. The measure is what gets built and who gets hired after we leave.

Compliance

Bonding, insurance & compliance

Backed well past the size of the job

Counterparties in this business are only as good as what stands behind them. Our capacity is sized so that the obligation we accept is fully covered for its whole life, not just through construction.

  • $2.4B aggregate surety and bonding capacity
  • High-limit pollution legal liability and contractor's policies
  • Licensed abatement, hazmat and blasting personnel in-house
  • Federal, state and municipal programme experience
