Who we are

Small enough to answer the phone

Beltway Demolition is a demolition and site clearance contractor working across Maryland, Washington DC and Virginia. We handle the stage of a project most people would rather not think about — the part where a building has to come down, the ground has to be made safe, and somebody has to keep the paperwork straight.

The company is backed by WMB LLC, which holds a construction contractor licence in the State of Maryland. That is what allows us to act as contractor of record on licensed work, and it is the first document we hand a client who asks.

You will deal with the same person from the site walk to the final invoice. On a job where the surprises are usually underground, that matters more than an org chart.

How we operate

Four things we do not cut corners on

Safety before schedule

A structure comes down in a planned sequence or it does not come down that day.

Permits first

Applications, utility disconnects and notifications are closed out before a machine is on site.

Keep the record

Disposal tickets, manifests and certificates go in your file, not just ours. A clean record is what makes a site sellable later.

Leave it clean

Graded, swept and walked with you before we call it finished.
