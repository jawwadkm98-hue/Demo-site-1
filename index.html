Liability Risk Transfer

An idle plant is a cost centre. We take it off your books.

Carrying an idle facility means carrying its taxes, security, insurance, monitoring and regulatory exposure indefinitely. Our risk transfer structure moves title and obligation in a single transaction, with the remediation funded and bonded from day one.

  • Fixed-price certainty in place of an open-ended reserve
  • High-limit environmental policies and $2.4B bonding capacity
  • Regulator engagement and closure documentation handled end to end
  • Community and workforce transition planned alongside the schedule

How we work

From first walkdown to a marketable parcel

01 / Assess

Diligence & valuation

Site walkdown, environmental review, salvage inventory and a hard number on what closure actually costs.

02 / Transfer

Structure the deal

Title, indemnity, insurance and bonding assembled into one agreement your board and counsel can sign.

03 / Execute

Decommission & clean

Abatement, demolition, recovery and remediation performed by our own crews and equipment, on our own schedule.

04 / Reposition

Return it to use

Entitlement, utilities and site work that turn a closed plant into industrial, energy or logistics-ready land.
