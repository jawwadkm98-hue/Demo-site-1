What we do

Demolition and site services across Maryland

Take it down, clear it, hand it back clean. Most jobs need more than one trade before anything new can be built. We handle the sequence end to end, so you are not managing three contractors and the gaps between them.

Demolition

Full and selective demolition: houses and garages, commercial buildings and interior strip-outs, including work alongside occupied buildings.

Hazardous Materials Coordination

Site Clearance & Grading

Licensing & backing

Licensed in Maryland, backed by WMB LLC

Beltway Demolition is a division of WMB LLC, which holds Maryland Home Improvement Commission license #154299 and Maryland Home Builder Registration #9597. That backing is what lets us take on work that needs a licensed contractor of record, and it is the first thing we will put in writing for you.

  • MHIC #154299 and MD HBR #9597 through WMB LLC
  • Certificates of insurance issued before mobilization
  • Disposal tickets and manifests kept for every load
  • One point of contact from quote to closeout
