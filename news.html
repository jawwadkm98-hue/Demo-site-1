Meridian acquires Riverbend Generating Station through risk liability transfer

The 640 MW station retired in 2023 after six decades of operation. Under the agreement, Meridian assumes title, closure obligations and long-term environmental responsibility, and will retain the switchyard to support future industrial load on the site.
