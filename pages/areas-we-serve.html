Regional detail

What each region is built for

Northeast & New England

Legacy mills, paper and textile complexes, and river-frontage industrial land where redevelopment competes with flood and historic-structure constraints.

Mid-Atlantic

Refining, chemical and glass manufacturing corridors. Dense permitting environments and high-value logistics reuse.

Southeast

Textile and furniture plant closures, plus a steady pipeline of retiring fossil generation across the Carolinas and Tennessee Valley.

Great Lakes & Midwest

Steel, automotive and heavy fabrication. Large parcels with rail and water access — our strongest market for energy reuse.

Gulf Coast

Petrochemical, terminal and marine assets. Tank farms, process units and dock structures with complex closure requirements.

Mountain West & Pacific

Mining, smelting and remote generation sites where logistics and site access drive the schedule more than the structures do.

Canada & international

Selective engagements, typically alongside an owner or lender with an existing relationship and a multi-site portfolio.
