Why it is structured this way

Self-perform, or you are just passing the risk along

A contractor who subcontracts the abatement, the haulage and the remediation cannot give an owner a fixed price on closure — because they do not control the three things most likely to move it.

Owning the companies means the group carries that exposure itself. The scale followed from the structure: to self-perform across a continent you need crews, yards and plant in every region, and once you have them the economics only work at volume.

  • One contract and one indemnity, not a chain of them
  • Salvage value credited internally rather than lost to a third party
  • One safety programme and one reporting standard across every site
