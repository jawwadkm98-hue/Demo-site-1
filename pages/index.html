Liability Risk Transfer

An idle plant is a cost centre. We take it off your books.

Carrying an idle facility means carrying its taxes, security, insurance, monitoring and regulatory exposure indefinitely. Our risk transfer structure moves title and obligation in a single transaction, with the remediation funded and bonded from day one.

  • Fixed-price certainty in place of an open-ended reserve
  • High-limit environmental policies and $600M bonding capacity
  • Regulator engagement and closure documentation handled end to end
  • Community and workforce transition planned alongside the schedule
