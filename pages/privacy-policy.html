Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. How this site handles the information you send us.

Overview

This policy explains what information this website collects, how it is used, and the choices available to visitors.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide. Details submitted through the enquiry form, such as name, company, email address, phone number and the description of a site or project.
  • Technical information. Standard server log data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, where the hosting provider records it.
  • Cookies. This site sets no tracking or advertising cookies.

How information is used

Information submitted through this site is used to:

  • respond to enquiries and evaluate potential projects;
  • maintain records of correspondence relating to a transaction; and
  • keep the website secure and functioning.

We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers who host the site or handle email on our behalf, with professional advisers where a project requires it, and where disclosure is required by law.

Retention

Enquiry records are kept for as long as needed to evaluate and, where applicable, perform the work — and afterwards for the period required by contract, insurance or regulation.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request a copy of your personal information, ask for corrections, or ask that it be deleted. Requests can be sent to info@beltwaydemo.com.

Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is entirely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Children

This site is intended for business use and is not directed at children.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to info@beltwaydemo.com or to the head office address listed on the contact page.
