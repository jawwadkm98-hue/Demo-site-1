Sector detail

What each sector demands

Power Generation

Coal, oil and gas stations, switchyards and transmission assets. The group's largest sector by volume, and the one where retained infrastructure most often carries value into the next use.

Oil, Gas & Petrochemical

Refineries, terminals, tank farms and process units. Closure here is driven by product inventory, vapour control and the state of the containment underneath.

Steel & Heavy Manufacturing

Mills, foundries, forges and fabrication halls. Heavy structural steel recovery usually offsets a material share of the takedown cost.

Automotive

Assembly plants, stamping and component works. Large clear-span buildings on well-served land, which makes them the group's strongest redevelopment candidates.

Pulp & Paper

Mills with river frontage, hydro assets and a century of accreted structures. Historic-structure and flood constraints usually shape the plan more than the demolition does.

Mining & Minerals

Smelters, processing plant, headframes and tailings infrastructure, generally remote and logistics-constrained.

Federal & Defense

Base closures and federal facility decommissioning, delivered under federal acquisition rules through Meridian Federal Services.

Ports & Marine

Docks, wharves, cranes and offshore structures, including in-water work alongside operating berths.

Chemical Processing

Batch and continuous plant where line purging, chemical cleaning and waste characterisation dominate the schedule.

Commercial & Institutional

Hospitals, campuses, hotels and towers — selective and full demolition in occupied, dense settings.
