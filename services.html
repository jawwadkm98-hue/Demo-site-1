What we do

Our demolition and site services

Each one is available on its own. Most jobs use several, in this order.

Demolition

Full and selective demolition of commercial, industrial and residential structures.

  • Structural demolition, full or partial
  • Interior strip-outs in occupied buildings
  • Garages, outbuildings, barns and slabs
  • Work next to neighboring buildings and occupied spaces

Residential   Commercial   Interior

Hazardous Materials Coordination

Asbestos, lead paint and other regulated materials have to be dealt with before a structure comes down. We do not perform abatement ourselves: we arrange the survey, bring in licensed abatement contractors and sequence their work ahead of demolition.

  • Pre-demolition hazardous-material surveys arranged
  • Abatement performed by licensed partner contractors
  • Tank removal and soil work referred to licensed specialists
  • Survey and clearance paperwork kept in your project file

Site Clearance & Grading

Everything between a demolished structure and a parcel a builder can start on.

  • Slab, footing and foundation removal
  • Grubbing, stump and vegetation clearance
  • Backfill, compaction and rough grading
  • Erosion and sediment control measures

Concrete & foundation removal

Asset Recovery & Recycling

Material worth more sold than dumped is recovered, and the value comes off the invoice.

  • Structural steel and non-ferrous recovery
  • Equipment, fixtures and architectural salvage
  • Concrete crushing and reuse where practical
  • Recycling records for LEED or client reporting

Permits & Project Management

The administrative work that stops a job before it starts, closed out in advance.

  • Demolition permit applications and inspections
  • Utility disconnect coordination
  • Regulatory notifications and waiting periods
  • Single point of contact from quote to closeout

Hauling & Disposal

Debris moved to permitted facilities, with the paperwork kept.

  • Debris, concrete and scrap haulage
  • Roll-off placement and exchange
  • Disposal at permitted and licensed facilities
  • Tickets and manifests retained for your file
