Capabilities

From the final shift to the first tenant

Everything between "we're closing this plant" and "something new opens here" — eight disciplines, self-performed and sequenced by one project team. Engage them individually, or hand us the whole asset.

Liability Risk Transfer

We purchase the property and assume the environmental, regulatory and post-closure obligations attached to it. The seller books a defined cost once, instead of carrying an open reserve for a decade.

  • Indemnity backed by insurance and surety
  • Powerplant and refinery decommissioning
  • Leveraged buyouts of distressed operating assets

Demolition & Implosion

Mechanical, high-reach and explosive demolition of process structures, silos, stacks and cooling towers — including work over live utilities, rail and adjacent operating plant.

  • Engineered takedown sequences and exclusion zones
  • Licensed blasting crews for implosion work
  • Selective and interior demolition in occupied facilities

Environmental Remediation

Asbestos and lead abatement, soil and groundwater remediation, tank and lagoon closure, and waste characterisation carried out to the standard the reviewing agency will actually accept.

  • Phase I / II assessment and remedial design
  • Excavation, treatment and licensed disposal
  • Long-term monitoring and closure documentation

Asset Recovery & Recycling

Equipment worth more running than scrapped is inventoried, tested and resold; the rest is processed through our own yards. Recovery value is credited straight back against the cost of the project.

  • Turbines, transformers, boilers and machine tools
  • Ferrous and non-ferrous processing
  • Managed industrial auctions and private sale

Construction Management

Owner's-representative delivery for clients who want the outcome without building a project team: budget, permits, procurement, subcontractor packages and closeout.

  • Feasibility, budgeting and scheduling
  • Permitting and agency coordination
  • Fee-based development services, domestic and international

Real Estate Development

Cleared, remediated land put back into the market — industrial and logistics parks, energy sites, yard and outdoor storage, office and mixed-use residential where the location supports it.

  • Entitlement, rezoning and incentive negotiation
  • Rail, power and utility infrastructure
  • Build-to-suit and long-term ground lease

Trucking & Equipment

An owned fleet of haul trucks, excavators, processors and support equipment — available to our projects first, and to third parties on bare rental or operated terms.

  • Bulk, debris and specialty hauling
  • High-reach and processing attachments
  • Rental with or without operators

Decommissioning & Idling

Safe shutdown, de-energising, draining, purging and mothballing of process plant — whether the asset is coming down next quarter or being held for a future buyer.

  • Chemical cleaning and line purging
  • Care, custody and site security programmes
  • Asset value assessment for shuttered facilities

Engagement

Three ways clients bring us in

Option A

As a contractor

Fixed-scope demolition, abatement or remediation, competitively bid and bonded. You keep the asset; we deliver the scope.

Option B

As a buyer

We acquire the facility and its liabilities outright. Your exposure ends at closing, at a price fixed before you sign.

Option C

As a partner

Joint venture on redevelopment, where you retain upside in the land and we carry the cleanup and the execution risk.

Scoping

Not sure which one fits?

Send the site details and we'll tell you which structure makes sense — and which doesn't.
