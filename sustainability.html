Community

The plant closes. The town does not.

When a facility shuts, the payroll goes first and the tax base goes with it. The group's transition programme hires from the workforce that ran the plant, into demolition, environmental and haul roles on their own site — and holds those jobs open through remediation.

Where the group retains land for redevelopment, local hiring commitments are written into the disposition agreement rather than left as an aspiration.

  • 2,180 former plant staff hired into group roles since FY21
  • Apprenticeships at 31 regional yards
  • Community liaison on every project over $25M
